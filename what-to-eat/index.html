About What to Eat

How it works

Keep the cuisines that sound good and rule out the rest. Ties are settled with head-to-head showdowns until one winner is left. Then we show real restaurants of that kind near a spot you pick.

Data

Restaurants come from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), fetched through the Overpass API. Addresses are searched with Nominatim. Map tiles are from tile.openstreetmap.org. Listings are only as complete and current as the volunteers who map them, so check hours before you go. Places labelled “(sample)” are made up for demos.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your location is only used when you press “Use my location”, search an address or tap the map, and it is sent only to Overpass, Nominatim and the map tile server. It never goes in a link unless you tick “Include my search location” when sharing.

Share this

Off by default. Turn it on and anyone with the link can see roughly where you searched.
